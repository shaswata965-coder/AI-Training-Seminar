# Requirements checklist: DIU AI training seminars

How each part of the brief for the website of the DIU AI Training Seminars, run by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering of Daffodil International University, is met.

| # | Brief | How it is met | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A website for the AI training seminars at Daffodil International University | Every page, default text, e-mail, PDF, social preview and admin label speaks of the **DIU AI Training Seminars**, organised by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, at Daffodil Smart City. Times are Bangladesh Standard Time (Asia/Dhaka). Nothing names, links to or borrows from any other institute's site; identifiers in the code (cookies, caches, the default database `diu-ai-training`) are the site's own. | `src/lib/content/defaults.ts`, `src/lib/schema/page-copy.ts`, `src/lib/format.ts` |
| 2 | Trainers lead every session | The **Trainers** page (`/trainers`), collection, admin entries, home carousel, schedule links and texts. Trainer fields: designation, affiliation, label (*DIU faculty*, *Industry expert*, *Guest trainer*), focus area, profile, areas of expertise, what they teach, lead trainer. Session types read *Opening*, *Lecture*, *Discussion*, *Hands-on lab*, *Demo*. | `src/app/(site)/trainers`, `src/components/trainers`, `src/lib/schema/resources.ts` |
| 3 | Profiles of the trainers from the department's list, with a hero profile | The nine people on the Department of CSE's list are built-in trainers, in its order. Professor Dr. Sheak Rashed Haider Noori (the list's convener) is the **lead trainer**, shown in a large spotlight at the top of the Trainers page; everyone has a card and a pop-up profile. Photos, designations and profiles are added in the admin panel. | `DEFAULT_TRAINERS` in `defaults.ts`, `components/trainers/TrainerSpotlight.tsx` |
| 4 | **Custom training**, no batches, no dates, no single course | Every training is designed for the organisation that asks for it, around its industry, its office work and its professional tasks, with efficiency gains as the aim: *Custom training* and *AI training designed around your organisation* on the home page's band and poster, *Custom, for your team* in the hero, a *Sample plan* (Day 1, Day 2…) instead of a schedule, FAQs such as *Is there a fixed course?* and *How long is a training, and when does it take place?*, and search engines told it is an **EducationalOrganization** of DIU (JSON-LD), not a course with dates. The site shows **no dates** anywhere: no next-batch dates, deadlines, reply-by dates, calendar links or `.ics` invites, announcement dates or completion dates, and the copyright line has no year. Sites set up with the batch-era texts get the new ones where an organiser never edited them (`BATCH_ERA_DEFAULTS` in `retired-batches.ts`, read through `RETIRED`), and their unedited default FAQs and sample session are updated once (`refreshBatchEraContent` in `bootstrap.ts`). | `page-copy.ts`, `defaults.ts`, `retired-batches.ts`, `bootstrap.ts`, `lib/jsonld.tsx`, `lib/training.ts` |
| 5 | **AI for professionals**, not agentic AI | Nothing describes autonomous AI agents any more. The About page's interactive loop shows how a professional works with AI on a real task: *Frame* the task, *Brief* the AI without sharing confidential data, let it *Draft*, *Check* facts and figures (your judgement decides), *Apply* and reuse the prompt; Aura's "thinker" look and the Meet Aura page follow it. Internal names no longer say *agentic*, *workshop* or *event* either (e.g. `registrationStatus`, `programmeName`, `learningText`, the `{programme}` placeholder, the `/api/admin/export/registrations` download, `/recordings` for slides and recordings, reference numbers like `DIUAI-7K3QX`); settings stored under the earlier names are read under the new ones (`withCurrentNames` in `defaults.ts`). | `components/home/WorkflowLoop.tsx`, `lib/content/defaults.ts`, `lib/training.ts` |
| 6 | No "coming soon" posters | Every empty state and placeholder says what happens instead of "coming soon" or "to be announced": every training is *planned with you*, rooms are *confirmed by e-mail*, prices are *on request*, volunteers get an *Open call*, partners *Be one of our first partners*, past participants *Your team could be here*, and so on. Sites set up with earlier wording get the new texts where an organiser never edited them (`RETIRED` in `defaults.ts`). | `page-copy.ts`, `defaults.ts`, `components/tutorials`, `components/programme` |
| 7 | **Contact us**, not *Register*; focus areas, not *two programmes* | Every *Register* / *Register now* button reads **Contact us** and leads to the training request form at `/contact` (`/register` redirects there). Content is grouped by two **focus areas**, *AI for Professional Work* and *AI for Office Work*, that a training can mix: home page cards with photos (*What we train*), filters on the Sample plan and Hands-on labs pages, a focus area on every session, lab, trainer and participant, a placard and a Contact us card for each in the home hero, and *What should the training focus on?* on the form. No "two programmes, one goal" wording. | `THEME_KEYS` in `constants.ts`, `components/home/Themes.tsx`, `RegisterChooser.tsx`, `src/app/(site)/contact`, `next.config.ts` |
| 8 | A **past participants** page | `/past-participants`: numbers (participants, trainings, organisations, focus areas), featured quotes, everyone training by training with filters and search, and a call to contact us. A home page section shows up to three quotes. | `src/app/(site)/past-participants`, `components/participants`, `components/home/ParticipantsTeaser.tsx` |
| 9 | Photos of Daffodil International University | The home hero shows DIU's academic building at Daffodil Smart City (its "Daffodil International University" sign above Aura's head) in the light theme and the Knowledge Tower by night in the dark theme, both from Wikimedia Commons and credited in the footer. They also illustrate the focus-area cards and the custom-training poster. The DIU logo and the Department of CSE seal sit in the navigation bar and the social preview; Aura wears the DIU shield. | `public/hero`, `public/themes`, `public/posters`, `public/logos`, `docs/IMAGE_CREDITS.md` |
| 10 | The **map location** | The venue's map and every *Open map* / *Directions* link point at a **map pin** (latitude, longitude) at DIU's main buildings, 23.8770, 90.3200, instead of relying on how Google reads a search text; the pin is editable (Site settings → Venue → The venue). A custom embedded map showing a place outside Bangladesh is ignored. The home page's route map of north-west Dhaka is re-framed with the campus away from the edge (its label was cut off on phones), at its true position, with the Birulia–Akran road, Khagan and DIU Road into campus. | `components/venue/links.ts`, `MapEmbed.tsx`, `dhaka-map.ts` |
| 11 | **Ask Aura** in the bottom-right corner | A button on every page (or Ctrl/⌘ + K) opens a search over the pages, home sections, FAQs, trainers, sessions and labs, with the venue and *Contact us* at hand. On by default; Site settings → Site-wide → Features & past trainings switches it off. The back-to-top button sits above it. | `components/aura/AuraAssistant.tsx`, `lib/content/search.ts` |
| 12 | A **more masculine Aura**, and a way to change his look | Aura's new **Strong** build: a square-jawed helmet with ear modules, an antenna and a chin grille, a visor with a brow, a neck joint, a broad V-shaped torso with a chest plate, armoured shoulder pads and arms ending in fists (a necktie instead of the bow tie when he lectures). He is "he" throughout. **Site settings → Site-wide → Aura** chooses the build (Strong or the original Classic), the colours (steel, DIU blue, graphite, classic white) and the eyes (a colour per section, or one everywhere); the choice applies to every Aura on the site, the logo, the loading screen and the social preview. The Meet Aura playground tries every combination. | `components/aura/builds.ts`, `AuraArt.tsx`, `AuraHead.tsx`, `MiniAura.tsx`, `app/layout.tsx` |

## Still to be confirmed by the organisers

These ship as reasonable defaults; change them in the admin panel before launch.

- The **pricing** (shown as "On request"), the **room**, contact e-mail and phone.
- The trainers' **designations**, photos and profiles, and who leads each focus area.
- The **sample plan**: the built-in sessions are an example of a two-day training covering both focus areas.
- The **directions** to Daffodil Smart City and the DIU transport routes, and the accommodation suggestions.
- **Past participants**: none are built in. `npm run seed:demo` adds clearly labelled samples to preview the page (`-- --remove` deletes them).
