# Requirements checklist: DIU AI training seminars

How each part of the brief for the website of the DIU AI Training Seminars, run by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering of Daffodil International University, is met.

| # | Brief | How it is met | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A website for the AI training seminars at Daffodil International University | Every page, default text, e-mail, calendar invite, PDF, social preview and admin label speaks of the **DIU AI Training Seminars**, organised by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, at Daffodil Smart City. Times are Bangladesh Standard Time (Asia/Dhaka). Nothing names, links to or borrows from any other institute's site; identifiers in the code (cookies, caches, the default database `diu-ai-training`) are the site's own. | `src/lib/content/defaults.ts`, `src/lib/schema/page-copy.ts`, `src/lib/format.ts` |
| 2 | Trainers rather than speakers | The **Trainers** page (`/trainers`), collection, admin entries, home carousel, schedule links and texts. Trainer fields: designation, affiliation, label (*DIU faculty*, *Industry expert*, *Guest trainer*), programme, profile, areas of expertise, what they teach, lead trainer. Session types read *Opening*, *Lecture*, *Discussion*, *Hands-on lab*, *Demo*. | `src/app/(site)/trainers`, `src/components/trainers`, `src/lib/schema/resources.ts` |
| 3 | Profiles of the trainers from the department's list, with a hero profile | The nine people on the Department of CSE's list are built-in trainers, in its order. Professor Dr. Sheak Rashed Haider Noori (the list's convener) is the **lead trainer**, shown in a large spotlight at the top of the Trainers page; everyone has a card and a pop-up profile. Photos, designations and profiles are added in the admin panel. | `DEFAULT_TRAINERS` in `defaults.ts`, `components/trainers/TrainerSpotlight.tsx` |
| 4 | **Continuous workshops**, not a single event | The training is presented as running all year, batch after batch: *Now enrolling* and *New batches start all year round* on the home page's enrolment band and poster, *Enrolling now* on Aura's placards and the registration cards, rolling enrolment on the Registration page (*register any time, and we'll place you in the next batch with free seats*), a *When does the next batch start?* FAQ, the next batch's dates wherever dates show, and search engines told it is a **Course** with a scheduled batch (JSON-LD). There is no "opening soon" status, countdown or year in the logo: registration is **open** or **paused**, and a site that stored "opening soon" reads as open. No talks, panels, poster session, kiosks or exhibitor forms. | `page-copy.ts`, `defaults.ts`, `constants.ts`, `lib/jsonld.tsx` |
| 5 | No "coming soon" posters | Every empty state and placeholder says what happens instead of "coming soon" or "to be announced": the schedule is *e-mailed before your batch*, rooms are *e-mailed before your batch*, fees are *on request*, volunteers get an *Open call*, partners *Be one of our first partners*, past participants *Your name could be here*, and so on. Sites set up with the earlier wording get the new texts where an organiser never edited them (`RETIRED` in `defaults.ts`). | `page-copy.ts`, `defaults.ts`, `components/tutorials`, `components/programme` |
| 6 | **AI for Professionals** and **AI for Workspace** | Home page cards with photos, filters on the Schedule and Hands-on labs pages, a programme on every session, lab, trainer and participant, a placard and a registration card for each in the home hero, and a *Which programme would you like to join?* question on the form. The schedule every batch follows: Day 1 AI for Professionals, Day 2 AI for Workspace. | `THEME_KEYS` in `constants.ts`, `components/home/Themes.tsx`, `RegisterChooser.tsx`, `DEFAULT_SESSIONS` |
| 7 | A **past participants** page | `/past-participants`: numbers (participants, batches, organisations, programmes), featured quotes, everyone batch by batch with filters and search, and a call to join the next batch. A home page section shows up to three quotes. | `src/app/(site)/past-participants`, `components/participants`, `components/home/ParticipantsTeaser.tsx` |
| 8 | Photos of Daffodil International University | The home hero shows DIU's academic building at Daffodil Smart City (its "Daffodil International University" sign above Aura's head) in the light theme and the Knowledge Tower by night in the dark theme, both from Wikimedia Commons and credited in the footer. They also illustrate the programme cards and the enrolment poster. The DIU logo and the Department of CSE seal sit in the navigation bar and the social preview; Aura wears the DIU shield. | `public/hero`, `public/themes`, `public/posters`, `public/logos`, `docs/IMAGE_CREDITS.md` |
| 9 | The **map location** | The venue's map and every *Open map* / *Directions* link point at a **map pin** (latitude, longitude) at DIU's main buildings, 23.8770, 90.3200, instead of relying on how Google reads a search text; the pin is editable (Site settings → Venue → The venue). A custom embedded map showing a place outside Bangladesh is ignored. The home page's route map of north-west Dhaka is re-framed with the campus away from the edge (its label was cut off on phones), at its true position, with the Birulia–Akran road, Khagan and DIU Road into campus. | `components/venue/links.ts`, `MapEmbed.tsx`, `dhaka-map.ts` |
| 10 | **Ask Aura** in the bottom-right corner | A button on every page (or Ctrl/⌘ + K) opens a search over the pages, home sections, FAQs, trainers, sessions and labs, with the next batch's dates, the venue and *Register* at hand. On by default; Site settings → Site-wide → Features & past batches switches it off. The back-to-top button sits above it. | `components/aura/AuraAssistant.tsx`, `lib/content/search.ts` |
| 11 | A **more masculine Aura**, and a way to change his look | Aura's new **Strong** build: a square-jawed helmet with ear modules, an antenna and a chin grille, a visor with a brow, a neck joint, a broad V-shaped torso with a chest plate, armoured shoulder pads and arms ending in fists (a necktie instead of the bow tie when he lectures). He is "he" throughout. **Site settings → Site-wide → Aura** chooses the build (Strong or the original Classic), the colours (steel, DIU blue, graphite, classic white) and the eyes (a colour per section, or one everywhere); the choice applies to every Aura on the site, the logo, the loading screen and the social preview. The Meet Aura playground tries every combination. | `components/aura/builds.ts`, `AuraArt.tsx`, `AuraHead.tsx`, `MiniAura.tsx`, `app/layout.tsx` |

## Still to be confirmed by the organisers

These ship as reasonable defaults; change them in the admin panel before launch.

- The next batch's **dates** (11–12 December 2026 is a placeholder), the **fee** (shown as "On request"), the **room**, contact e-mail and phone. Move the dates on as each batch starts.
- The trainers' **designations**, photos and profiles, and who leads each programme.
- The **schedule**: the built-in sessions are an outline of the two programmes.
- The **directions** to Daffodil Smart City and the DIU transport routes, and the accommodation suggestions.
- **Past participants**: none are built in. `npm run seed:demo` adds clearly labelled samples to preview the page (`-- --remove` deletes them).
