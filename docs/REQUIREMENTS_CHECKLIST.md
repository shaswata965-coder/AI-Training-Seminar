# Requirements checklist: DIU AI training seminars

How each part of the brief for the Daffodil International University version of the site is met. This version lives on its own branch (`claude/diu-ai-training`); the IIT Delhi Agentic AI Workshop site stays on its original branch.

| # | Brief | How it is met | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A second version of the website, for AI training seminars at Daffodil International University | Every page, default text, e-mail, calendar invite, PDF, social preview and admin label speaks of the **DIU AI Training Seminars**, organised by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, at Daffodil Smart City. Times are Bangladesh Standard Time (Asia/Dhaka). | `src/lib/content/defaults.ts`, `src/lib/schema/page-copy.ts`, `src/lib/format.ts` |
| 2 | Replace everything about speakers with **trainers** | The Speakers page, collection, admin entries, home carousel, schedule links and texts are now **Trainers** (`/trainers`). Trainer fields: designation, affiliation, label (*DIU faculty*, *Industry expert*, *Guest trainer*), programme, profile, areas of expertise, what they teach, lead trainer. Session types read *Opening*, *Lecture*, *Discussion*, *Hands-on lab*, *Demo*. | `src/app/(site)/trainers`, `src/components/trainers`, `src/lib/schema/resources.ts` |
| 3 | Profiles of the trainers from the attendance-sheet list, with a hero profile | The nine people on the Department of CSE's list are built-in trainers, in its order. Professor Dr. Sheak Rashed Haider Noori (the list's convener) is the **lead trainer**, shown in a large spotlight ("hero profile") at the top of the Trainers page with his programmes, profile and the faces of the team; everyone has a card and a pop-up profile below. Photos, designations and profiles are added in the admin panel. | `DEFAULT_TRAINERS` in `defaults.ts`, `components/trainers/TrainerSpotlight.tsx` |
| 4 | Remove everything about the hackathon | The Hackathon page, team registration form and API, admin editor, data model, settings, texts, navigation links, home and schedule sections, placard, FAQs, CSV export, sign-in by team ID, e-mails, mentors and judges, and the load-test scenario are gone. `/hackathon` is a 404. | removed throughout |
| 5 | Remove everything about the single event | The two-day workshop's talks, panels, poster session (call for posters and its form question), industry kiosks and *Exhibit with us* form, networking dinner and invitation texts are gone. The site now describes training **programmes** run in **batches**: the home page and schedule show the *next batch*, and past batches have their own page. | `defaults.ts`, `page-copy.ts`, `section-order.ts` |
| 6 | Content about AI training, such as **AI for Professionals** and **AI for Workspace** | The two programmes replace the workshop's three themes: home page cards with photos, filters on the Schedule and Hands-on labs pages, a programme on every session, lab, trainer and participant, a placard and a registration card for each in the home hero, and a *Which programme would you like to join?* question on the form (pre-ticked from the home page cards). A draft two-day schedule: Day 1 AI for Professionals, Day 2 AI for Workspace. About, FAQs and partnership texts are written for the training. | `THEME_KEYS` in `constants.ts`, `components/home/Themes.tsx`, `RegisterChooser.tsx`, `DEFAULT_SESSIONS` |
| 7 | A **past participants** page | `/past-participants`, in the navigation bar: numbers (participants, batches, organisations, programmes), featured quotes (*What participants say*), everyone batch by batch with filters by programme and batch and a search, and a call to join the next batch. Participants are added under **Past participants** in the admin panel (name, designation, organisation, programme, batch, date, optional photo, quote and profile link). A home page section shows up to three quotes. | `src/app/(site)/past-participants`, `components/participants`, `components/home/ParticipantsTeaser.tsx` |
| 8 | Replace the IIT Delhi background image with a photo of Daffodil International University from the internet | The home hero shows DIU's academic building at Daffodil Smart City (its "Daffodil International University" sign above Aura's head) in the light theme and the Knowledge Tower by night (its lit sign) in the dark theme, both from Wikimedia Commons and credited in the footer. They also illustrate the programme cards and the registration poster. | `public/hero`, `public/themes`, `public/posters`, `docs/IMAGE_CREDITS.md` |
| – | Branding that follows from it | The DIU logo and the Department of CSE seal (from the department's own documents) replace the IIT Delhi emblem and the ScAI logo in the navigation bar and the social preview; Aura wears the DIU shield; the route map is a schematic of north-west Dhaka (airport, MRT Line 6, the Turag, routes to Daffodil Smart City). | `public/logos`, `components/layout/InstituteLogo.tsx`, `components/venue/dhaka-map.ts` |

## Still to be confirmed by the organisers

These ship as placeholders or reasonable defaults; change them in the admin panel before launch.

- The next batch's **dates** (11–12 December 2026 is a placeholder), **fees** ("To be announced"), **room**, contact e-mail and phone.
- The trainers' **designations**, photos and profiles (the attendance sheet gives names and committee roles only), and who leads each programme.
- The **schedule**: the built-in sessions are a draft outline of the two programmes.
- The **directions** to Daffodil Smart City and the DIU transport routes, and the accommodation suggestions.
- **Past participants**: none are built in. `npm run seed:demo` adds clearly labelled samples to preview the page (`-- --remove` deletes them).
